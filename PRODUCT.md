# Ritatu Product

Ritatu is a mobile macro tracker for people who want fast meal logging without giving up control over weight and nutrition assumptions. The app serves everyday nutrition tracking, not medical diagnosis.

Register: product

## Users

- People tracking calories and macros daily.
- Users who search products, scan packaged products, photograph meals, or log a quick kcal value.
- Users who need quick correction before save, especially portion weight.

## Product Principles

- The user confirms weight (or kcal for a quick entry) before every meal is saved.
- Calories come from label energy when a product has it, otherwise from protein, carbs and fat (4/4/9). AI estimates ingredients and grams; code sums the totals.
- Logging must feel fast, clear, and trustworthy: every save is confirmed, every delete can be undone or asks first when it cannot.
- No dead ends: a failed search or scan points to another way to log (search, scan, photo, quick kcal, custom product).
- A meal is saved to the day the diary shows, whichever add path the user takes.
- Days under the user's optional kcal floor are treated as not fully logged and kept out of averages.

## Tone

Calm, practical, warm, and precise. Plain Polish, no formula names or jargon in the UI. The app should feel like a focused daily tool with small moments of delight, not a marketing site.

## Anti-References

- No generic purple gradients.
- No heavy dark dashboard look.
- No gamified clutter.
- No hidden assumptions about nutrition data.
